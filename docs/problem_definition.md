# AutoShield AI

## Problem

Automotive manufacturers face disruptions due to:

- Supplier delays
- Geopolitical risks
- Material shortages
- Logistics bottlenecks
- Demand volatility

Current ERP systems react after disruptions occur.

## Solution

AutoShield AI predicts disruptions before they occur and recommends mitigation actions.

## Users

- Supply Chain Managers
- Procurement Teams
- Manufacturing Planners
- Plant Heads

## Key Outputs

- Supplier Risk Score
- Inventory Risk Score
- Demand Forecast
- Alternative Supplier Recommendations
- What-if Scenario Analysis
